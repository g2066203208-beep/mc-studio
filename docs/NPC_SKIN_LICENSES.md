# NPC Skin Licenses (v0.5.5)

The six packaged skin PNG textures under `mod/src/main/resources/assets/reme_studio/textures/entity/npc/` are original skins from the free [Ross's Minecraft Skins](https://github.com/raws/minecraft-skins) collection, authored by **Ross Paffett**, Copyright (c) 2022. Original images `casual.png`, `indy.png`, and `tuxedo.png` are MIT licensed; they have been adapted to 64×64 Java UV format and three color variants derived from them. This is not Mojang's Steve texture.

### Skin sources considered but not bundled

- [ThatOneSillyGuy's Skin Pack (CurseForge, Creative Commons 4.0)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/skins/yet-another-generic-skin-pack) is artist-authored and was reviewed, but its package download returned HTTP 403 in our test runner, so it was **not** incorporated. A third-party character in that pack also has a separate named creator, needing explicit rights verification.
- The Skindex/NameMC/ordinary Minecraft skin websites often allow free **personal download** but do not automatically license third-party redistribution. We do **not** scrape and redistribute those without permission.
